# Transactions

Route `/transactions`, a tab. Screen `src/modules/transactions/screens/transactions/index.tsx`, month row from `src/components/ui/month_filter.tsx` over the type tabs from `src/components/ui/segment_filter.tsx`, their spacing and hit slops from `components/transactions_rail.geometry.ts`, hero `components/transactions_hero.tsx`, filter sheet `filter/index.tsx` over the shared `src/components/ui/filter_accordion.tsx`. Frames A1 to A6, A10 to A14, A16, B1 and B2 come from the transactions canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/7QJH3AFeoQAXtWmx5vAP2s, epic #543), not `~/.ship/MoneyApp/canvas/`, whose B1 and B2 are the accounts list.

## Reach it

- User path: the `Transactions` tab, or `See all` in an account detail's activity card.
- Script: `mqa open /transactions`, or the tab bar's exact content-desc from `mqa ui`; the bare `$MQA tap 'Transactions'` is refused under the uiautomator engine.
- The filter sheet opens from the tune icon beside the search field: `$MQA tap` by its accessibility label `Filter` (`Strings.filterSearchButtonAccessibility`; `Filter, N active`, `Strings.filterAccessibilityWithActiveCount`, once a filter is on).

## Gotchas

- `month_filter.tsx` is shared with commitments and budget; a height read here holds there, and a divergence is a caller override.
- The `hero, change against last month` seed puts rows two and three months back, so on that device `hero, empty month` and `hero, past month` read differently: the first empty month over an empty month before it is four back, and one back shows a change node.
- `mqa ui` returns nothing while the hero skeleton shimmers: `uiautomator dump` waits for an idle screen. Read the search field through `adb shell dumpsys activity top` instead.
- A source-forced totals failure logs through `console.error`, so the dev LogBox toast sits over the tab bar in the shot; it is not the screen's alert.
- This list's group headers are `DayHeader` (`components/day_header.tsx`), not the shared `section_header.tsx` nor `DateHeader`, which only the commitments list still renders; the `section title` state belongs to `dashboard.md` and `accounts_list.md`. The header root is one accessibility node, so its label, net and pill `TextView`s may not appear in `mqa ui`: read the content-desc, and the capitals from the shot.
- `TypeBadge` renders at `sm` in the row and `md` in the detail hero; `md` is measured on `transaction_detail.md`.
- The compact `SegmentedTabs` is shared with the accounts-list rail and the transaction form's type tabs; one read holds on all three, and a divergence is a caller override.
- `adjustsFontSizeToFit` can shrink a long compact label below 11 px. Read a short label for the line box, and judge a shrunk one against the compact track, which follows `resolveSegmentedTabsGeometry(fontScale).compact.triggerHeight`. `minimumFontScale` is iOS-only: on Android `adjustsFontSizeToFit` shrinks down to a 4 dp floor.
- No `hitSlop` shows in `mqa bounds`, so a hit area is proven by a tap off the drawn control, never by a node's box. The type tabs' `HorizontalScrollView` reads 4 dp above and 7 dp below the 34 track by design: the track is the list node (`label="Transaction type filter"`), and the scroll box is grown to hold the triggers' slop (MA-109).
- The pill's height is `resolveMonthPillGeometry(fontScale).height`: 32 at 1.0, growing once the label's line box and its padding pass 32 (MA-126).
- An account chip and the filter sheet's account pill share the label `<account>, account filter`, so with the sheet open `tap 'label="<account>, account filter"'` matches both; pick the sheet's pill by `tapxy` on the centre of the match whose `bounds` sit below the chip row, or by its `@ref` from `mqa read` (MA-120). With the sheet closed the label matches the chip alone.
- The search field is disabled while the hero skeleton shows on a month's first load, so the `search tally, skeleton` state is reached with an account applied through `See all`, never by typing (MA-121).
- A tap on a chip past the first scrolls the chip row and `All accounts` leaves the screen, where `mqa tap 'label="All accounts"'` finds no match; a second tap on the selected chip returns the selection to `All accounts` (MA-107).
- The search `EditText` answers to `label="Search transactions"` only while it is empty; with text in it, its label is the text. Tap `Clear search` before the next `fill` (MA-121).
- A deep link does not dismiss an open bottom sheet: the filter sheet stays mounted over the next screen and its nodes answer the reads. `am force-stop` before the next state.
- With the delete dialog open, `mqa read` prints none of its text. `mqa bounds '~cannot be undone'` prints the dialog's group node, whose label joins the title, the body and the failure line. The swipe tile and the dialog's confirm button both answer to `label="Delete"`: the dialog's is the match 48 dp high, tapped by `tapxy` on its centre (MA-093).
- `filtered empty clear link, large font` (MA-130): the search fill reaches `No results in <month>` at both scales. The first `mqa scroll` after it, with `keyboard: shown`, leaves the tab for Home (a Back), so it is the scroll, not the typing. The clear link sits below the keyboard: at 1.0 it reads y=647.2, 11.05 dp visible (MA-109, read with no keyboard on the `Transfer` tab's `No results`; 657.1 and 1.14 under the carded rail). The link is the same `EmptyState` `filtered` variant measured whole at 2.0 in `commitments.md` and `accounts_list.md`.
