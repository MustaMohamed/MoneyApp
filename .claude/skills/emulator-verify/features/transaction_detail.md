# Transaction detail

Route `/transactions/detail/[id]`, and its `/stacked` twin when reached from a stacked screen. Screen `src/modules/transactions/screens/transactions/detail/index.tsx`, hero `detail/components/detail_hero.tsx` over the shared `src/components/ui/status_badge.tsx`, sized by `resolveDetailHeroGeometry` in `detail/components/detail.geometry.ts`; fact rows `detail/components/detail_fact_row.tsx` drawn over the shared `src/components/ui/fact_row.tsx`, at `resolveFactRowHeight` (`src/components/ui/fact_row.geometry.ts`), the note `note_card.tsx`, the actions `action_row.tsx`. Frames C1 to C13 come from the transactions canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/7QJH3AFeoQAXtWmx5vAP2s, epic #543), not `~/.ship/MoneyApp/canvas/`. The hero follows them since MA-180. Since MA-182 (#723) the failure states and the delete follow C8, C9, C11 and C12: the not-found state as shipped, the first-load error block, the delete on the list's dialog and the refresh alert. Since MA-192 the hero amount is in the transaction's own currency, and the Amount in EGP and Amount in USD rows replace the Original amount row. Since MA-181 (#722) the facts are rows with the label left and the value right, each label as written and not in capitals, the note sits in its own card, and the actions are flat. Since MA-196 (#757) the transfer flow card, `detail/components/transfer_flow_card.tsx`, draws each account on its own tile, a deleted or unknown side on the graphite hollow tile the Account row draws, and its skeleton only while the account lookup is in flight. Since MA-252 (#851) the not-found state, the load error and the header pencil are the shared `src/components/ui/detail_not_found_state.tsx`, `src/components/ui/detail_load_error.tsx` and `src/components/ui/detail_edit_action.tsx`, which both details mount.

## Reach it

- User path: a transaction row on the `Transactions` tab (`transactions.md` § Outbound).
- Script: `$MQA tap '<transaction title>'` on the list.
