# Transaction detail

Route `/transactions/detail/[id]`, and its `/stacked` twin when reached from a stacked screen. Screen `src/modules/transactions/screens/transactions/detail/index.tsx`, hero `detail/components/detail_hero.tsx` over the shared `src/components/ui/status_badge.tsx`, sized by `resolveDetailHeroGeometry` in `detail/components/detail.geometry.ts`; fact rows `detail/components/detail_fact_row.tsx` at `resolveDetailFactRowHeight`, the note `note_card.tsx`, the actions `action_row.tsx`. Frames C1 to C7, C10 and C13 come from the transactions canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/7QJH3AFeoQAXtWmx5vAP2s, epic #543), not `~/.ship/MoneyApp/canvas/`. The hero follows them since MA-180. Since MA-192 the hero amount is in the transaction's own currency, and the Amount in EGP and Amount in USD rows replace the Original amount row. Since MA-181 (#722) the facts are rows with the label left and the value right, each label as written and not in capitals, the note sits in its own card, and the actions are flat. The transfer flow card stays as on `main` until MA-196 (#757).

## Reach it

- User path: a transaction row on the `Transactions` tab (`transactions.md` § Outbound).
- Script: `$MQA tap '<transaction title>'` on the list.
