# Transaction form

The add and edit sheet, over any tab. Host `src/modules/transactions/screens/transactions/transaction_form/index.tsx`, mounted once at `src/app/(app)/_layout.tsx`; body `transaction_form_body.tsx`, account strip `components/account_strip.tsx`, type tabs `components/type_tabs.tsx` over the shared `src/components/ui/tabs.tsx`, skeleton `components/transaction_form_loading.tsx`, footer status track `src/components/ui/status_track.tsx`. Frames D1 to D8, D13 to D15, A15 come from the transactions canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/7QJH3AFeoQAXtWmx5vAP2s, epic #543), not `~/.ship/MoneyApp/canvas/`, whose D frames are the edit-account screen.

## Reach it

- Add: the FAB on the Transactions or Dashboard tab, `$MQA tap 'Add'` (`fab.tsx:213`), then the menu pill `$MQA tap 'Add Transaction'`.
- Add from an account: the account detail's add path (`account_detail.hook.ts:381`, `openAdd({ accountId })`) opens the sheet after a jump to the Transactions tab, the account preselected.
- Edit: a transaction row on the list (`transactions.md` § Outbound), then the detail header's edit icon, `$MQA tap 'Edit transaction'` (`detail_header.tsx:34`, `Strings.detailEditAccessibility`).
- Edit from a list row: swipe the row left from inside it, `adb -s <serial> shell input swipe 800 <row centre px> 100 <row centre px> 400`, the centre from `mqa bounds` on the row's label, then `$MQA tap 'label="Edit"'` (`transaction_row.tsx`, `Strings.swipeEdit`).

## Gotchas

- The detail's edit icon label (`Strings.detailEditAccessibility`) equals the sheet title in edit, so `grep -c 'Edit transaction'` over `mqa read` on the detail counts one before the sheet opens and two after. The FAB menu pill reads `Add Transaction` (title case, `fab.tsx:143`), the sheet title `Add transaction`: grep the sheet title case-sensitively.
- The compact `SegmentedTabs` is shared with the list's type tabs and the accounts-list rail (`transactions.md` § Gotchas); one read of a trigger holds on all three, and a divergence is a caller override.
- The fact rows are `ListGroup.Item` pressables: they are the clickable nodes, so `mqa bounds` on the row's testID, not on its key or value text. A 44 dp row reads 43.81 or 44.19 (115 or 116 px): one device pixel of rounding, not a short row.
- The skeleton's containers are not in the tree: the root's `Loading transaction` label folds them. With the skeleton on screen the agent-device snapshot does not settle either: a `tap` or `read` fails after 30 s (`Android snapshot helper failed`) and `bounds` then reads `no match` even for `Save`. Prove the loading state from the shot, close the sheet with `tapxy` on its close button, and for layout numbers log `e.nativeEvent.layout` from a temporary `onLayout` and read it with `mqa logs --info` (console.log is info level).
- The form's scroll offset survives a type-tab switch: close and reopen the sheet before shooting a later state, or the To row sits above the viewport and reads 0 by 0.
- The skeleton needs the opening status forced, not the loader: once the account and category stores have loaded (any tab does it), `getOpeningState` opens the sheet `ready` and `loadPrerequisites` never runs, so a never-settling loader shows nothing.
- Never `mqa ime-down` with the sheet open under the agent-device engine: the keyboard is headless, the key is Back, and Back closes the sheet. `fill` the amount and tap on.
- The type tab triggers share their labels with the list's filter rail behind the sheet (`Income`, `Transfer`), so `tap 'Income'` is ambiguous; tap the sheet's trigger by its position (`tapxy`, from `mqa bounds 'label="Income"'`, the match at y 259).
- Under the agent-device engine, one `mqa scroll down` with a text field focused leaves the sheet stuck: Save writes nothing and close does nothing. Blur the field first, or run the walk under `MQA_UI=uiautomator`.
- The dev-client Tools button is off after `mqa up`; after `mqa reset` it returns, and `mqa park` turns it off again.
- The strip chip is the 118 by 40 row the sheet frames draw, the tile beside the name, ruled 2026-09-27 (MA-122) over the canvas base rule's 64-wide column. A chip that reads 64 wide is the defect. Its vertical hit slop is `(44 − ms(40)) / 2 + ms(1)` (`Spacing.xxxxs`), 2.07 dp on `Pixel_2_API_34`: at the bare 1.02 dp a tap 0.76 dp above the top edge missed in two runs. On the locked edit chip the lock glyph takes the name's room: `Walk Bank` draws `Walk B…` and `Deleted Account` `Delete…`, while the label carries the whole name.
- List drags in the sheet: read `date-row` and `transaction-form-scroll` bounds in the same walk; for the up drag start at `date-row`'s centre and end 95 dp higher; for the drag back start at the centre of a fact row read after the up drag and end 95 dp lower; convert at 2.625 px per dp on `Pixel_2_API_34`; drag with `adb -s <serial> shell input swipe 540 <start px> 540 <end px> 600`. No list drag is longer than 95 dp, and a longer travel is the short drag repeated: once the list reads its first row, gorhom's release snap counts the whole travel plus a fifth of the release velocity, and past half the sheet's height it closes the sheet, about 165 dp in 300 ms on `Pixel_2_API_34` (`node_modules/@gorhom/bottom-sheet/src/hooks/useGestureEventsHandlersDefault.tsx:378-416`, `node_modules/@gorhom/bottom-sheet/src/utilities/snapPoint.ts:7`). Fixed coordinates miss: after a cold `mqa up` the sheet drew 20 dp lower than after an `mqa seed` relaunch (list top 532.6 against 512.4). `mqa scroll --in 'id="transaction-form-scroll"'` starts its drag at 80% of the box, inside the footer, which takes the touch.
- `Food & Dining` is ambiguous once the category picker is open: the list row behind the sheet carries the same text. Tap the picker cell by its testID, `id="category-picker-cell-cat_food"`.
- The account detail's add path: `mqa wait 'Add transaction'` passes on the account detail before the tab jump. Wait on `label="Save"`, which only the sheet draws.
- A strip swipe that starts at x 1000 px, 30 dp from the right edge, can land as the Back edge gesture: start the drag at x 800 px. Two such swipes with the add sheet open close it, and `reopen after two edge swipes` is the proof that the next open draws the full form.
- After the add or edit sheet closes over the Transactions list, `Search transactions` holds focus (focus border, caret, and `mqa state` reads `keyboard: shown` under the headless keyboard); it predates MA-106 and MA-133 (#617) owns it. A walk that types next blurs the field first.
- The keyboard's top is the y of the `touchable region` on the InputMethod window in `adb -s <serial> shell dumpsys window windows`: 1048 px, 399.2 dp on `Pixel_2_API_34`. A region that starts at 1794 px is the navigation bar alone, and an empty one (`SkRegion()`) means no keyboard draws.
- Before a keyboard state read `adb -s <serial> shell settings get secure default_input_method`. When it still names `com.callstack.agentdevice.imehelper` after `mqa down`, the headless keyboard is selected under uiautomator too: `mqa state` reads `keyboard: shown`, nothing draws, the sheet does not lift and `mqa back` closes the sheet. `mqa up` followed by `mqa down` put the system keyboard back on 2026-10-04; a reboot alone did not.
- In that headless state, Note focus then `mqa back` leaves an `Add transaction` sheet drawn with its title and no body or footer, over the FAB, 2 of 2 runs at `638c69d8`; with the system keyboard the same walk reopens whole. MA-123 (#598) owns it.

## Seeding and forcing states

Per `README.md` § Seeding and forcing states. The To picker states need one bank account, one second non-card account and one credit card; the edit state needs one expense on the bank account; `add, USD account` needs one USD bank account; `add, strip scrolls` needs six or more active accounts; `edit, transfer` needs one transfer between two non-card accounts; `edit, deleted account` needs one expense on an account row soft-deleted (`is_deleted = 1`, `name = ''`); `edit, deleted counterparty` needs one transfer from the bank account to a second soft-deleted account; `no accounts` needs every account row archived (`is_archived = 1`), none deleted. Build them on a copy of `mqa seed --save` with `better-sqlite3` and push it with `mqa seed`.
