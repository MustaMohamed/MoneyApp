# Transaction form

The add and edit sheet, over any tab. Host `src/modules/transactions/screens/transactions/transaction_form/index.tsx`, mounted once at `src/app/(app)/_layout.tsx`; body `transaction_form_body.tsx`, account strip `src/components/ui/account_strip.tsx`, type tabs `components/type_tabs.tsx` over the shared `src/components/ui/tabs.tsx`, skeleton `components/transaction_form_loading.tsx`, fact rows `src/components/ui/form_picker_row.tsx` over the shared `src/components/ui/fact_row.tsx`, the Note cell `src/components/ui/note_fact_row.tsx` over the shared fact row, footer status track `src/components/ui/status_track.tsx`. Frames D1 to D8, D13 to D15, A15 come from the transactions canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/7QJH3AFeoQAXtWmx5vAP2s, epic #543), not `~/.ship/MoneyApp/canvas/`, whose D frames are the edit-account screen.

## Reach it

- Add: the FAB on the Transactions or Dashboard tab, `$MQA tap 'Add'` (`Strings.fabAddAccessibility`), then the menu pill `$MQA tap 'Add Transaction'`.
- Add from an account: the account detail's add path (`account_detail.hook.ts:381`, `openAdd({ accountId })`) opens the sheet after a jump to the Transactions tab, the account preselected.
- Edit: a transaction row on the list (`transactions.md` § Outbound), then the detail header's edit icon, `$MQA tap 'Edit transaction'` (`detail_header.tsx:34`, `Strings.detailEditAccessibility`).
- Edit from a list row: swipe the row left from inside it, `adb -s <serial> shell input swipe 800 <row centre px> 100 <row centre px> 400`, the centre from `mqa bounds` on the row's label, then `$MQA tap 'label="Edit"'` (`transaction_row.tsx`, `Strings.swipeEdit`).

## Seeding and forcing states

Per `README.md` § Seeding and forcing states. The To picker states need one bank account, one second non-card account and one credit card; the edit state needs one expense on the bank account; `add, USD account` needs one USD bank account; `add transfer, USD destination` needs one EGP bank account and one USD non-card account; `rate stale` needs the `add, USD account` seed with `usd_rate_updated_at` in `app_settings` more than 30 days back; `edit, rate differs from stored` needs the `add, USD account` seed, `usd_rate` 52.137035 in `app_settings`, and one USD expense on the USD account saved at `exchange_rate` 49 with its `egp_amount` at 49 times its amount; `add, strip scrolls` needs six or more active accounts; `edit, transfer` needs one transfer between two non-card accounts; `edit, deleted account` needs one expense on an account row soft-deleted (`is_deleted = 1`, `name = ''`); `edit, deleted counterparty` needs one transfer from the bank account to a second soft-deleted account; `no accounts` needs every account row archived (`is_archived = 1`), none deleted. Build them on a copy of `mqa seed --save` with `better-sqlite3` and push it with `mqa seed`.
