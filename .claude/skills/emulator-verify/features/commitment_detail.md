# Commitment detail

Route `/commitments/[id]?cycle=<payment id>` on the tab stack from the list, and its `/stacked` twin with `originTxId` from `View commitment` on a transaction; `[id]` is the commitment id. Screen `src/modules/commitments/screens/commitments/detail/index.tsx`, current-cycle card `detail/components/current_cycle_card.tsx`, state components `detail/components/detail_skeleton.tsx`, and the shared `src/components/ui/detail_not_found_state.tsx`, `src/components/ui/detail_load_error.tsx` and `src/components/ui/detail_edit_action.tsx` since MA-252. Frame ids are the commitments canvas's (https://claude.ai/artifact/E94FBgEhKRXmP2NB8zL6vX). Since MA-260 the hero is the shared `src/components/ui/detail_hero.tsx` the transaction detail also draws, `id="commitment-detail-hero"` on its body, sized by `resolveDetailHeroGeometry` (`src/components/ui/detail_hero.geometry.ts`) and following frames C1 to C5: the 48 dp category tile, the name, the cadence badge, `Amount` with the amount, and the caption. Since MA-261 the facts are the shared `src/components/ui/detail_fact_row.tsx` rows in `id="commitment-detail-facts"`, each `resolveFactRowHeight` high (`src/components/ui/fact_row.geometry.ts`), built by `buildCommitmentDetailFacts` (`detail/detail.helpers.ts`), and the note is the shared `src/components/ui/detail_note_card.tsx`, `id="commitment-detail-note-card"`; the transaction detail draws both too. Only the cycle card and payments are not redesigned yet (MA-262) and draw as they are today.

## Reach it

- User path: a commitment row on the `Commitments` tab (`commitments.md` § Outbound).
- Script: `$MQA tap '<commitment name>'` on the list; the row pushes `/commitments/<commitment id>?cycle=<payment id>` (`commitments.hook.ts:311-313`).
- Deep link: `$MQA open '/commitments/<commitment id>?cycle=<payment id>'`; the detail reads the commitment and its rows from the repository, so it opens before any month has loaded.

## Gotchas

- The pill is a `View` with no touch handler, so it is flattened out of the accessibility tree: read the label `TextView` and add `py-0.5` (2 dp each side), or measure the painted pill on the shot (`README.md` § The rule).
- `Mark as paid` is the only way to `Paid` without a seed push, and it does not come back — use one commitment per status, or re-push the seed.
