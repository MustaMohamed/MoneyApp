# Commitment detail

Route `/commitments/[id]?cycle=<payment id>` on the tab stack from the list, and its `/stacked` twin with `originTxId` from `View commitment` on a transaction; `[id]` is the commitment id. Screen `src/modules/commitments/screens/commitments/detail/index.tsx`, current-cycle card `detail/components/current_cycle_card.tsx`, state components `detail/components/detail_skeleton.tsx`, and the shared `src/components/ui/detail_not_found_state.tsx`, `src/components/ui/detail_load_error.tsx` and `src/components/ui/detail_edit_action.tsx` since MA-252. Frame ids are the commitments canvas's (https://claude.ai/artifact/E94FBgEhKRXmP2NB8zL6vX). Since MA-260 the hero is the shared `src/components/ui/detail_hero.tsx` the transaction detail also draws, `id="commitment-detail-hero"` on its body, sized by `resolveDetailHeroGeometry` (`src/components/ui/detail_hero.geometry.ts`) and following frames C1 to C5: the 48 dp category tile, the name, the cadence badge, `Amount` with the amount, and the caption. Since MA-261 the facts are the shared `src/components/ui/detail_fact_row.tsx` rows in `id="commitment-detail-facts"`, each `resolveFactRowHeight` high (`src/components/ui/fact_row.geometry.ts`), built by `buildCommitmentDetailFacts` (`detail/detail.helpers.ts`), and the note is the shared `src/components/ui/detail_note_card.tsx`, `id="commitment-detail-note-card"`; the transaction detail draws both too. Since MA-262 the This cycle card, `id="commitment-detail-cycle"`, follows frames C1 to C5: the `THIS CYCLE` overline, the `tagMd` `StatusBadge`, the due day, the day line, the amount and its code, then `Pay` and `Skip` on an unpaid cycle, `View transaction` on a paid cycle with a transaction and no action row otherwise, built by `buildCommitmentCycleCard` (`detail/detail.helpers.ts`). Since MA-265 the Payments card follows frames C1 to C5: `detail/components/payment_history.tsx` draws `SectionHeader` over `DetailRowsCard`, `id="commitment-detail-payments"`, and draws nothing with no row; `detail/components/payment_row.tsx` draws each row `buildCommitmentPaymentRows` (`detail/detail.helpers.ts`) returns, the three latest due before today, newest first, the shown cycle left out; the pay sheet's cycle line, `id="pay-sheet-cycle-line"`, sits beside the `tagMd` tag in `FormTypeTabsRow`, the row the add-transaction sheet's type tabs sit in. Since MA-267 the This cycle card and the skeleton's cycle card, `id="commitment-detail-skeleton-cycle"`, share `resolveCurrentCycleCardGeometry` (`detail/components/current_cycle_card.geometry.ts`): the loaded card holds its height as a `minHeight` and the skeleton card stands at it as its `height`, each with room for the action row only where the cycle has actions (`commitmentCycleShowsActions`, `detail/detail.helpers.ts`), and the skeleton keeps that room when the store holds no copy of the opened cycle. A paid cycle with its transaction stands at `resolveCurrentCycleCardGeometry(scale, true).height` and one without at `resolveCurrentCycleCardGeometry(scale, false).height`, by `commitmentCycleShowsActions`, which `__tests__/screens/commitments_detail.helpers.test.ts` checks against `buildCommitmentCycleCard`; the device rows prove both heights on the unpaid and skipped cycles. Since MA-266 the bottom Edit row, `id="commitment-detail-actions"` (`detail/components/detail_actions.tsx`), holds a flat secondary `Edit` last in the scroll, the row `transaction_detail.md` reads as `detail-actions`; the cadence badge reads `Stopped` or `Ended` by `resolveCommitmentLifecycle` (`src/modules/commitments/domain/payment_state.ts`), with the clock glyph and the cadence colours; the This cycle card shows on an active commitment, never on a stopped one, and on an ended one only at the cycle the detail opened at unpaid (`commitmentDetailShowsCycleCard`); the bottom Edit and the header pencil show only on an active commitment (`commitmentDetailShowsEdit`); and the skeleton draws its cycle card and its rows only where the loaded detail keeps them (`resolveCommitmentDetailSkeletonCards`), the hero shell always.

## Reach it

- User path: a commitment row on the `Commitments` tab (`commitments.md` § Outbound).
- Script: `$MQA tap '<commitment name>'` on the list; the row pushes `/commitments/<commitment id>?cycle=<payment id>` (`commitments.hook.ts:375-377`).
- Deep link: `$MQA open '/commitments/<commitment id>?cycle=<payment id>'`; the detail reads the commitment and its rows from the repository, so it opens before any month has loaded.

## Gotchas

- The `tagMd` `StatusBadge` is a `View` with no touch handler, so it is flattened out of the accessibility tree: read the label `TextView` and add `STATUS_BADGE_PADDING_VERTICAL` (2 dp each side), or measure the painted tag on the shot (`README.md` § The rule).
- `Pay` is the only way to `Paid` without a seed push, and it does not come back: use one commitment per status, or re-push the seed. A seed push that reaches `Paid` gives the row the transaction a Pay writes, by the `Paid row` rule of `commitments.md` § Seeding and forcing states (MA-246).
- The card's `Pay` (MA-262) and the pay sheet's empty-amount `Pay` (MA-259) share `label="Pay"`, unwalked together: if a tap in the open sheet reports more than one node, press the sheet's `@ref` from `mqa read`.
- A detail deep-linked over the Home tab sits in the Commitments tab's stack: `mqa back` and the header `Go back` land on the Dashboard while that tab keeps the detail mounted, so the same URL opened again lands on the open detail with its held state, and an ended commitment has no row on the month list to reach it by (MA-266). A recipe that needs the detail closed before it opens the URL again runs `adb -s <serial> shell am force-stop com.moneyapp.app` and a cold launch (`mqa up`) first.
