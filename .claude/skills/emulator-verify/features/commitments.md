# Commitments

Route `/commitments`, a tab. Screen `src/modules/commitments/screens/commitments/index.tsx`, month row from `src/components/ui/month_filter.tsx` over the status tabs from `src/components/ui/segment_filter.tsx`, their spacing and hit slops from `src/components/ui/list_rail.geometry.ts`, the transactions tab's rail (MA-241); hero `components/commitments_hero.tsx` on `src/components/ui/list_hero.tsx`; account chips from `src/components/ui/account_chips.tsx`; search and filter row `components/search_row.tsx`; tally from `src/components/ui/search_tally.tsx`; filter sheet `filter/index.tsx` in the shared `src/components/ui/filter_sheet_frame.tsx`, with the shared Accounts, Categories and Amount sections `filter_account_accordion.tsx`, `filter_category_accordion.tsx` and `filter_amount_accordion.tsx` over the shared `src/components/ui/filter_accordion.tsx`, and its Commitments section `filter/components/commitment_accordion.tsx` (MA-213). The rail, the hero, the chips, the search row, the tally and the list's sections and rows follow the commitments canvas (#777: A1, A2ChipOn, A3Search, A11PastMonth), and the filter sheet its B1 and B2.

## Reach it

- User path: the `Commitments` tab.
- Script: `mqa open /commitments`, or the tab bar's exact content-desc from `mqa ui`; the bare `$MQA tap 'Commitments'` is refused under the uiautomator engine.
- The filter sheet opens from the tune icon beside the search field: `$MQA tap` by its accessibility label `Filter` (`Filter, N active` once a filter is on).

## Gotchas

- The count pill draws only at `count > 0`: an accordion with nothing selected has no pill to measure.
- `filter_accordion.tsx` is shared with the transactions filter sheet; a height read here holds there, and a divergence is a caller override.
- `filter_sheet_frame.tsx` and the Accounts, Categories and Amount sections are shared with the transactions filter sheet (MA-233); a read of the frame or a section here holds there, and a divergence is a caller override.
- With a section open, `label="<Title>"` matches two nodes, the header button and its title `TextView`. A walk closes an open section with `tapxy` on the title's centre, which lies inside the button (MA-233).
- A section header's button box holds the card's vertical padding, and the 12 dp gap above the body while open: 47.24 to 47.62 dp closed and 45.71 open at 1.0, 64.38 to 64.76 and 62.86 at 2.0. Its title `TextView` sits 14 dp under the button's top (MA-233).
- At 2.0 with the filter sheet at its 45% snap, the lower option pills sit under `sheet-footer`, and a tap on one lands on Reset. Drag the sheet to 92% before a pick (MA-233).
- The accordion pill has no vertical padding, so its height is the line box alone — 14, not 18 like the padded pills.
- The month pill in the rail is the shared `month_filter.tsx`, measured once as `month filter pill` in `transactions.md` § States; that read holds here, so do not re-measure it — a divergence is a caller override.
- This list's section headers are the shared `DayHeader` and its rows sit in the shared `DayCardRow` slices (MA-209), measured once in `transactions.md` as `day headers, all accounts` and `day card, all accounts`; a read there holds here, and a divergence is a caller override. The `section title` state belongs to `dashboard.md` and `accounts_list.md`.
- The row status pill is a `View` with no touch handler: it is flattened out of the accessibility tree, so read the label `TextView` and add 2, or measure the painted pill on the shot.
- `FilterOptionPillList` renders the shared `SelectablePill` and is the same component the transactions filter sheet renders; the adornment branch is measured here and the adornment-free branch on `add_commitment.md`, one read each.
- `~Filter` matches seven nodes and `~Apply` can land behind the sheet: use `label="Filter"`, `label="Filter, 1 active"`, `label="Apply"` or `label="Apply (1)"`. After `mqa seed` the app opens on the Dashboard, so `mqa open /commitments` comes before any read of the list.
- A deep link does not dismiss an open bottom sheet: the filter sheet stays mounted over the next screen and its nodes answer the reads. `am force-stop` before the next state.
- With the search field focused after a `mqa fill`, `mqa scroll` lands on Home, at 1.0 and at 2.0 (MA-214). Reach a block below the fold with `adb -s <serial> shell input swipe 150 1300 150 300 600`, left of the + button.
- First load on the populated seed, 28 commitments and 1,545 payments, load near 5: skeleton from +6 s, hero +15 s, rows +17 s after `mqa open` (MA-214). `mqa wait` defaults to 10 s, so a walk names a longer wait.
- A drag down from `sheet-header` ends on screen (MA-214): an `input swipe` ending past the display (y=2175 on 1920 px) left the FAB gone and the next tap dead, one ending at y=1900 closes the sheet.
- HeroUI's toast root lays a padding-high placeholder over its top and bottom after its children (`node_modules/heroui-native/src/components/toast/toast.tsx:215-222`), so a control in a toast takes no tap inside the root's padding until it draws above them, which `TOAST_ACTION_Z_INDEX` (`src/components/ui/toast.tsx`) does. On a toast forced persistent at 1.0, `Undo` took a tap 4 px past its box and none at 8 px with the root's `overflow: hidden` and with `overflow: visible`; the clip does not bound the band, the placeholders do (MA-215).
- The tab bar's clickable node carries the icon glyph before the label (`B, Budget` on Budget), so `$MQA tap 'Commitments'` matches only the non-clickable text and is refused; tap the exact content-desc from `mqa ui`, or use the deep link.

## Seeding and forcing states

See `README.md` § Seeding and forcing states.

**Paid row** (MA-246), the rule every recipe here that seeds a paid row cites: the seed holds what a Pay writes, `markCommitmentAsPaid` (`src/modules/commitments/database/commitment_payments.ts:345-401`) in one write transaction, with the figures `markAsPaid` resolves (`src/modules/commitments/repositories/commitment.repository.ts:209-261`):

- The payment: `paid_date` the paid day, `amount_paid` the amount paid in the commitment's currency, `account_id` the paid-from account, `exchange_rate_snapshot` the rate when either currency is USD, and `transaction_id` the transaction's `id`.
- One `transactions` row: `type` `expense`, `account_id` the paid-from account, `transaction_date` the paid day, `amount` and `currency` in that account's currency, `egp_amount` and `exchange_rate` as `resolveCommitmentPaymentAmounts` (`src/modules/transactions/domain/transaction_amounts.ts:161`) gives them for the commitment's and the account's currencies, `category_id` the commitment's, and `commitment_payment_id` the payment's `id`. On an EGP commitment `egp_amount` is the amount paid; on a USD commitment it is the amount paid times the rate, through `roundMoney`.
- The paid-from account's `current_balance` moved by that expense, as `resolveCreateDeltas` (`src/modules/transactions/domain/transaction_policy.ts:233`) gives it for the account's type.

`commitment_payments.transaction_id` references `transactions(id)` (`src/database/migrations/022_drop_payment_status.ts:25`), so the transaction goes in before the payment names it. A paid row with no transaction counts 0 in its section's sum, the tally and the hero (`sumCommitmentRowsEgp`, `src/modules/commitments/domain/commitment_section_sums.ts:32-34`, `docs/adr/2026-10-22-commitments-section-sums.md` §1), a state no app write produces. A recipe may instead pay the row in the app, `Mark as Paid` on the commitment detail, and keep the device state with `mqa seed --save` (README, Seed push).
