# Add commitment

No route: one sheet host for add and edit, `src/modules/commitments/screens/commitments/commitment_form/index.tsx`, mounted at `src/app/(app)/_layout.tsx`, opens over any tab and over the commitment detail with the body `src/modules/commitments/screens/commitments/components/commitment_form_body.tsx`. Add opens from the FAB; edit opens over the list (a row's `Edit` swipe), the tabbed detail and the stacked detail, with the Deactivate link under the fields and its confirm `commitment_form/components/deactivate_sheet.tsx`. The title reads `New Commitment` on add and `Edit Commitment` on edit. The Recurrence and Duration groups (`components/recurrence_picker.tsx`, `components/duration_picker.tsx`) render the shared `SelectablePill` from `src/components/ui/chip.tsx`. The top half, the amount type tabs, the account strip and the amount hero, is the redesign's (#777, MA-221); the rest is not redesigned by #378 and is drawn as it is today. This file carries only the pill states MA-087 needs — add the rest when a ticket reaches them.

## Reach it

- User path: on any tab, a long press on the FAB (`Add`) opens its menu, then its `Add Commitment` item; a tap on `fab-button` opens the transaction form instead (`src/components/ui/fab.tsx`, `onFABPress`). Or the commitments empty state's `Add Commitment`.
- Script: `adb -s <serial> shell input swipe 540 1581 540 1581 800`, a long press at `fab-button`'s centre in px on the 411 dp emulator at 1.0 and at 2.0, then `$MQA tap 'id="fab-menu-item-2"'` and `$MQA wait 'id="sheet-header"'`. Every row whose force opens the add sheet from the FAB takes this recipe; there is no `mqa open` link to it.
- Edit, from the list: `$MQA open /commitments`, `$MQA wait 'text="TOTAL COMMITTED"'`, then `$MQA scroll down --until '~<name>, <status>'` (`~Internet, Overdue`), since at 2.0 the row sits below the fold; then the row's swipe as `commitments.md` § States `row, swipe with a commitment`, then `$MQA tap 'Edit'`. From the detail: `$MQA tap '~<name>'` on `/commitments`, then `$MQA tap 'label="Edit commitment"'`, the header's pencil (`Strings.commitmentsDetailEditAccessibility`). From the stacked detail: `transaction_detail.md` § States `commitment-owned`, then `$MQA tap 'View commitment'`, then `$MQA tap 'label="Edit commitment"'`. Each waits on `$MQA wait 'text="Edit Commitment"'`.
- A scroll inside the sheet is `--in 'id="commitment-form-scroll"'`, or the list drag of `transaction_form.md` § Gotchas where that file bars `--in`.

## Gotchas

- `SelectablePill` is shared with the commitments and transactions filter sheets (`FilterOptionPillList`, `filter_accordion.tsx`). Its two container branches are measured once each: the adornment-free branch here (`px-3 py-1`, 25) and the adornment branch on `commitments.md` (`px-2.5 py-1.5`, 29). Both numbers include `SelectablePill`'s own 1 dp `border` pair, which the label-plus-padding arithmetic alone misses. A divergence is a caller override.
- The pill's `Chip` carries `accessibilityRole="button"`, so unlike a padded badge it reaches the accessibility tree and is measured directly.

- MA-220: under agent-device, `mqa tap`, `mqa scroll` and `mqa back` first send Back while a field holds focus (`ime-down`), and the headless keyboard passes that Back to the sheet, which closes. After a `fill`, blur with a plain `adb -s <serial> shell input tap` on a field label inside the form, and tap `Save Commitment` by `adb` at its centre from `mqa bounds`. A real keyboard takes that Back itself.
- MA-220: at 2.0, `label="Category"` matches the `Category` button and its `CATEGORY` caption; tap the button at its centre from `mqa bounds` instead.
- MA-220: the lift row's keyboard top is the `InputMethod` window's `touchable region` top in `adb -s <serial> shell dumpsys window windows`, 1048 px (399.2 dp) at 1.0. With the keyboard up, `sheet-footer` read 301.7 to 399.2, Name 104.4 to 152.4 and Notes 229.7 to 301.7, so Notes ends at the footer's top.
- MA-239: a typed-then-blurred field followed by `mqa scroll` leaves the host at `closing` with the sheet drawn; Save, Deactivate and Back are then dead for that session. Blur by `adb` tap and do not `mqa scroll` after typing.
- MA-239, the lift row: under `MQA_UI=uiautomator` a `scroll --until 'text="Add a note (optional)"'` stops at once, since the dump lists Notes while it sits under the footer. Bring Notes into view under agent-device (`$MQA scroll down --in 'id="commitment-form-scroll"' --until 'label="Deactivate Commitment"'` on edit), read its centre from `mqa bounds`, then `mqa down` and `MQA_UI=uiautomator $MQA tapxy` there. After `mqa down`, agent-device needs `$MQA open <route>` before its next verb, and a uiautomator `tap 'id="sheet-close-btn"'` after the keyboard went down hit a stale y and missed; close under agent-device.
- MA-254: the type tabs, the support line and the strip row are `FormTypeTabs` (`src/components/ui/form_type_tabs.tsx`), drawn by the add and edit transaction sheet too (`transaction_form.md`); one read holds on both sheets, and a divergence is a caller override. Here the support line wraps; on the transaction sheet it is one line.
