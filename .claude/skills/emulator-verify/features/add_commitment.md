# Add commitment

No route: one sheet host for add and edit, `src/modules/commitments/screens/commitments/commitment_form/index.tsx`, mounted at `src/app/(app)/_layout.tsx`, opens over any tab and over the commitment detail with the body `src/modules/commitments/screens/commitments/components/commitment_form_body.tsx`. Add opens from the FAB; edit opens over the tabbed detail and the stacked detail, with the `Stop this commitment` link under the rows card and its confirm `commitment_form/components/deactivate_sheet.tsx`. The title reads `Add commitment` on add and `Edit commitment` on edit. The facts are one rows card, `commitment-form-fact-group`, the transaction sheet's (MA-255): `name-row`, `category-row`, `repeats-row`, `starts-row` (the shared `DateRow`), `ends-row`, `currency-row` and `note-row`. `repeats-row` and `ends-row` each open a titled sheet, `repeats-sheet` and `ends-sheet` (`components/commitment_choice_sheet.tsx`). The Repeats sheet (`components/repeats_sheet.tsx`, MA-276) holds four option rows in one card, `repeats-option-weekly`, `-monthly`, `-annually` and `-custom` (`CommitmentChoiceOptions`, a HeroUI `RadioGroup` whose rows are the shared `RadioOptionRow`, `src/components/ui/radio_option_row.tsx`, MA-279, whose trailing radio has no node of its own in the accessibility tree), and on Custom the `repeats-custom` block: the shared stepper `repeats-stepper` (`src/components/ui/stepper.tsx`; `-decrease`, `-value`, `-increase`), the unit choice `repeats-unit` and the line `repeats-custom-line`. Its footer is the shared `FormSheetFooter`, `repeats-sheet-status` over a flat `Done`, and only Done writes the row; above font scale 1 it scrolls in `repeats-sheet-scroll`. The Ends sheet (`components/ends_sheet.tsx`, MA-277) holds three option rows in one card, `ends-option-forever`, `-after_count` and `-until_date`; on After the `ends-after` block holds the shared stepper `ends-stepper` with its unit `ends-stepper-unit` inside its border, and on a date the `ends-date` block holds one card with the shared `DateRow` `ends-date-row`, floored at Starts; each block ends with its preview line inside its border, `ends-after-line` and `ends-date-line` (MA-280). Its footer is `ends-sheet-status` over a flat `Done`, and only Done writes the row; above font scale 1 it scrolls in `ends-sheet-scroll`. Both blocks and the Repeats `repeats-custom` block are `CommitmentChoiceBlock`. The top half, the amount type tabs, the account strip and the amount hero, is the redesign's (#777, MA-221); the rows card is MA-255's, and the footer is MA-256's, `StatusTrack` `commitment-form-status` over the flat `Save`, as the transaction sheet draws them. The Repeats track and Custom line name the next due day, and the Ends line, track and row the last day or the count, all read from Starts: on a fresh add sheet Starts is today, T, and the rows below name their days from T, each `{day}` on the Repeats sheet as `Ddd d Mon` with its year in a year other than today's, and on the Ends sheet and row always with its year (MA-280). This file carries only the states its tickets reached — add the rest when a ticket reaches them.

## Reach it

- User path: on any tab, a long press on the FAB (`Add`) opens its menu, then its `Add Commitment` item; a tap on `fab-button` opens the transaction form instead (`src/components/ui/fab.tsx`, `onFABPress`). Or the commitments empty state's `Add commitment`.
- Script: `adb -s <serial> shell input motionevent DOWN 540 1581`, `sleep 1.2`, then `adb -s <serial> shell input motionevent UP 540 1581`, a long press at `fab-button`'s centre in px on the 411 dp emulator at 1.0 and at 2.0, then `$MQA tap 'id="fab-menu-item-2"'` and `$MQA wait 'id="sheet-header"'`. Every row whose force opens the add sheet from the FAB takes this recipe; there is no `mqa open` link to it.
- Edit, from the detail: `$MQA open /commitments`, `$MQA wait 'id="commitments-hero"'`, then `$MQA scroll down --until '~<name>, <account>'` (`~Internet, CIB Current Account`; a row with no account reads `<name>, <status>`), since at 2.0 the row sits below the fold; then `$MQA tap '~<name>'` (§ Gotchas, a row under the FAB), then `$MQA tap 'label="Edit commitment"'`, the header's pencil (`Strings.commitmentsDetailEditAccessibility`). From the stacked detail: `transaction_detail.md` § States `commitment-owned`, then `$MQA tap 'View commitment'`, then `$MQA tap 'label="Edit commitment"'`. Each waits on `$MQA wait 'id="sheet-header"'`, since the detail's pencil is also labelled `Edit commitment`.
- A scroll inside the sheet is `--in 'id="commitment-form-scroll"'`, or the list drag of `transaction_form.md` § Gotchas where that file bars `--in`.

## Gotchas

- The native date picker draws November 2026 at rows y 873, 978, 1083, 1188, 1293 and columns x 261, 353, 445, 537, 629, 721, 813 px, in a dialog from x 162 to 918 (MA-256). A tap on a blank cell or outside the grid leaves the pick unchanged, so a pick that does not land is a wrong tap until a shot of the dialog says otherwise.
- The native date picker's month arrows are in agent-device's tree: `$MQA tap 'label="Previous month"'` and `'label="Next month"'` (MA-277). Under a `minimumDate` no previous arrow draws in the month of that day, and its earlier days read greyed.
- A `fab-menu-item-2` tap while `Commitment added` shows does not open the sheet (MA-256): wait 4 s for the toast to go.
- A commitments list row under the FAB (MA-256): `mqa tap` presses the row's centre, x 540, and `fab-button` takes it at px (540, 1585). `mqa bounds` the row 2 s after the scroll stops, since a tap right after a raw swipe read a stale y, then `adb -s <serial> shell input tap 300 <y>`.

- MA-220: under agent-device, `mqa tap`, `mqa scroll` and `mqa back` first send Back while a field holds focus (`ime-down`), and the headless keyboard passes that Back to the sheet, which closes. After a `fill`, blur with a plain `adb -s <serial> shell input tap` on a field label inside the form, and tap `Save` by `adb` at its centre from `mqa bounds`. A real keyboard takes that Back itself.
- MA-255: the Category row draws no `CATEGORY` caption; `id="category-row"` is the one match at every scale.
- MA-220: the lift row's keyboard top is the `InputMethod` window's `touchable region` top in `adb -s <serial> shell dumpsys window windows`, 1048 px (399.2 dp) at 1.0. With the keyboard up, `sheet-footer` read 301.7 to 399.2, Name 104.4 to 152.4 and Notes 229.7 to 301.7, so Notes ends at the footer's top. At 2.0 on the edit sheet, the list dragged to `Stop this commitment` first, the Note's `EditText` read 117.0 to 198.5 over `sheet-footer` at 301.7 to 399.22 (MA-215).
- MA-239, the lift row: under `MQA_UI=uiautomator` a `scroll --until 'text="Add a note (optional)"'` stops at once, since the dump lists Note while it sits under the footer.
