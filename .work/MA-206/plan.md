## Self-assessment
Step 7 is the step I am least sure of. Turning `useTransactionRatePreview` into a converter changes a tested hook's signature, and it moves the editor's per-draft conversion from a memoised hook to a call on every render. The resolver is cheap and pure, but the editor body now rerenders on any change to `convertedFor`'s identity. The fix is the `useCallback` deps, and no test asserts render counts. Step 3's ~170 new lines are the other weak point, since the ~385 figure depends on GitHub pairing each `git mv` as a rename.
