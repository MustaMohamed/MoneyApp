## Risks
- A seam shows between slices of fractional height (`ms(60)` is 63.3 dp on the Pixel_2), or the side borders of adjacent slices do not meet: the ruling's fallback applies and the plan is amended.
- The swipe tiles draw square over the card's rounded corner; if the render pass reads that as wrong against the Acceptance line, the same fallback applies.
- The row is transparent over the slice's surface fill, so a half-open swipe shows the tiles through the row, as it does on the flat list today.
- `DayCardRow` is a plain `View`; if review reads Team Law 7 against it, the written justification is the ADR's §9.
- Jest's window `fontScale` may not be 1, so step 4's case reads the window's value, never `resolveDayHeaderGeometry(1)`.
- The card takes `2 × Spacing.md` + `2 × Size.hairline` from the row's width, about 36 dp at 411 dp wide. The value track is `flexShrink: 0` (`transaction_row.tsx:192`), so the title and the caption take the whole loss; a USD row with its rate line is the widest case.
- Amended 2026-09-28 for review round 1: the list's skeleton wiring asserted in the screen suite (step 4), the separator's proof stated (step 2), the width risk corrected with a USD proof, two filter states added to Screens; the count is ~185.

## Self-assessment
Step 3 is the one I am least sure about. The slice sets no `overflow` so the swipe tiles stay whole, and that leaves two things only the emulator shows: whether adjacent slices meet without a line at a 63.3 dp row height, and whether a square tile over a rounded corner is what the Acceptance line means by whole. The canvas clips its card (`.lgrp`, `overflow:hidden`), which the Acceptance line overrides. Both outcomes lead to the fallback the ruling names, so the risk is a second plan round, not a wrong build. Step 4's header bars take the day header's `lineHeight` and local widths, since the ticket fixes only the header row's height.
