## Self-assessment
Step 7's label-size half is the one I am least sure about. That no rail label draws under twice its 1.0 size rests on arithmetic: the `px-1.5` padding and the `Size.filterSegmentIcon` glyph stay fixed while the segment doubles, and Android's shrink loop binary-searches whole pixels, so a label that shrinks at 1.0 should settle at or above twice that size at 2.0. Hinting can move a glyph run by a pixel, which the proof's 2 px allowance absorbs. A label still under twice its 1.0 size on the device is a discrepancy, and its fix is the segment's width, not the label's props.
