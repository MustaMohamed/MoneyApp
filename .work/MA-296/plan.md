## Risks
- A `fitContent` sheet stacked over the `liftsAboveKeyboard` add sheet may settle wrong if the keyboard is still closing when it opens: `budget.md · set-budget sheet, group row`.
- The render suite's single module-level `onRadioValueChange` mock holds the last mounted `RadioGroup`; with the inline group gone the picker is the only one, but a second radio group mounted later would break the case.
- `budget.md · group picker, large font`: three captions wrapping at 2.0 could push a `fitContent` sheet past the screen; if so it needs `scrollable` with `BottomSheetScrollView`, as `resolveCommitmentChoiceSheetSizing` switches.
- `Keyboard.dismiss()` in a `.hook.ts` has no precedent in `src/`; the only call is `src/components/ui/sheet.tsx:118`.
- Step 6's `size="lg"` raises the set-budget sheet's top by 15% of the screen; a `budget.md` set-budget recipe that reads a rest position measured at `md` may read differently.

## Self-assessment
Step 5 is the least sure. The picker is a new composition of `RadioOptionRow` rows with two lines each, where the shipped one-choice pickers draw one line (commitments) or one line plus an amount (transactions budget picker). Whether a `fitContent` sheet holds three wrapped two-line rows at font scale 2.0 without clipping, stacked over a `liftsAboveKeyboard` sheet, is only provable on the emulator. A wrong sizing choice there would send the implementer back to the `scrollable` path that commitments uses for its footer sheets.
