## Self-assessment
Step 6 is the one I am least sure of: the hero's identity across a keystroke rests on three separate mechanisms holding at once (the store keeping `current`/`previous` identity on equal figures, `preserveData` being true on the keystroke path, and `'refreshing'` collapsing to `'figures'` in the mode resolver), and the `today` string is recomputed every render, so any one of them slipping shows up as a failing render-count test rather than a type error. The hook suite's first case in that step is written to catch exactly that, and it is the test the implementer should run first.
