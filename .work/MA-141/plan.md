  - When `CustomStyleSpan`s with a non-null `fontFamily` cover every character of `spanned`, no run reads the paint's face (`CustomStyleSpan.kt:70-71`, `ReactTypefaceUtils.kt:107-111`), and `t` is the face `CustomStyleSpan.updateMeasureState` leaves on a `TextPaint`.
