## Self-assessment
Step 2 is the one I am least sure of. The line-range mechanism puts the arithmetic on the dispatch: it writes its report, then has to give the first and last line of each section a verdict opens, and an agent at the end of a long run may count wrong. The fallback, a `grep -n '^## '` on the report, keeps the read bounded but costs a call each time. The report name `<role>-<sha>[-<k>].md` is a naming choice of mine, extending `findings/<lens>.md` at `SKILL.md:77`: it needs no counter across a resume, `-<k>` separates parallel fixers on one head, and it overwrites only on a re-dispatch at the same head, after the conductor has read the earlier verdict.
