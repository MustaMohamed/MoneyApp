## Risks
- The mechanism comes from reading the source and was not shown on the device. If a head launch blanks, step 2 is a discrepancy for `/prep --amend`, not a reason to retry.
- It is not yet shown on the device that the hero enters on Home's opening render. The probe read only through `mqa up` and `mqa seed`, which return after their ready wait, so at 20000 ms its first shot showed the rest position. That fits an entrance that ended before the shot and also a hero that never entered. Step 3's launch-time shot loop is the first read that can tell the two apart. If no opening shot shows the hero partly transparent, step 2 is a discrepancy for `/prep --amend`.
- The blanks came at a 1-minute load of 6.7, on the first two launches after a cleared Metro. On a quieter host, `main` may not blank, and the Acceptance needs its blank first. Step 3 caps the base walk at 30 launches, and a walk with no blank goes to `/ship` as a parked discrepancy with its load figures.
- Reading the ref in render keeps `babel-plugin-react-compiler` 1.0.0 off `useDashboardAnim` (`validateRefAccessDuringRender` defaults to `true`). If the compiler compiled the hook, it would memoize `heroEntering` and replay the entrance on every Overview return. Jest never runs the compiler, so only the `hero entrance` state's Overview return can catch it.
- `dashboard.md · hero on a cold launch, one account and one transaction`: the frame is rounded at 16 with `overflow: 'hidden'` (`src/components/ui/hero_shell.tsx:51-59`), and the band reads 163 dp, which may not be a whole number of dp. The head rest box must match `main`'s within 1 dp.
- `dashboard.md · hero entrance, on opening Home and back on Overview`: the rise moves a rounded, clipped frame through fractional dp offsets. A shot taken mid-rise must show its corners and border whole.
