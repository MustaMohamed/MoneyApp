## Self-assessment
Step 4 is the least certain. The second `useFocusEffect` keyed on month and account is the one mechanism that covers focus, month change and scope change without a mount double read, but it reshapes the hook test's focus mock and runs the aggregate read during the focus transition, without the `runAfterInteractions` deferral the rows load uses. Reading today in render, rather than carrying it on the slot, follows the transactions hook. Days left can trail the aggregate's own today across midnight until the next focus.
