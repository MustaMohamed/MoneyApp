## Self-assessment
Least sure about step 4's `reloadAccountActivityAfterDelete`. It decides that the detail sits over an account detail by reading the singleton activity store's snapshot. That holds because the stacked twin is pushed only from an activity row (`account_detail.hook.ts:361`) and the account detail resets the store on unmount, but it is a read of another screen's state rather than of the route the detail came from. Driving `ensure` rather than waiting on the store's status was chosen so the toast can never hang: `ensure` resolves on both outcomes, and the account detail's focus load joins the same request by key. If the render lens sees the card's skeleton under the toast, the defer through `runAfterInteractions` is the line to check.
