## Risks
- `useLocalToday(…, true)` keeps the day subscription while the detail sits blurred under another route; if the frozen stack still re-renders on a blurred day change, it is one extra render, not a stale figure.
- The focus callback depending on `currentMonth` re-runs `load` on a day change that moves the month; on the same month it does not, so the budget store's focus gate (`.claude/rules/state.md` 2) is untouched.
- `local_day_ticker.ts` is the only path that moves `currentMonth` while no render happens: a month crossed with neither a midnight nor a foreground notify leaves the focus callback loading the old month until the next render re-reads the seed.
- `formatDisplayAmountParts` escalates a sub-1 EGP figure to `0.40` where the tab's `formatAmount` prints `0`; the ADR records it, and a reviewer may ask for `formatAmount` instead.
- budget_category_detail.md · over the limit, S07: the seed's over month is a past month, so S07's tick and days left are not exercised on that state; the current month's tick is exercised by `current month, B1`.

- budget_category_detail.md · skeleton, large font: the bar row's tick overhang is outside the geometry, so the loaded hero and the skeleton can differ by the 3 dp overhang only if the shell clips; `DetailHero` draws no clip on the bar row (`detail_hero.tsx:71`).
