## Self-assessment
Step 2 is the one I am least sure about: replacing `resolveFlatRadius` with a resolver that also returns class names and a height puts three concerns in one function, and `button.tsx`'s three render branches each have to consume it without changing what the twenty-odd flat callers get today. The alternative, a second resolver just for the compact arm, keeps the diff smaller but leaves the danger radius and the accent fill decided in two places. The union arm and the `cn` merge are straightforward; the risk is a branch that forgets to apply `rootClass` and passes the test because the test reads the resolver, not the render.
