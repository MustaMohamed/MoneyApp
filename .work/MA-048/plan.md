## Risks
- The ticket header counts "four new strings"; step 1 adds seven (title, summary join, caption template, button, toast, clash, failure), every one named in the PR under the copy flag.
- Amended after review round 1: step 7 resets the card on unmount as well as at mount, since a mount-only effect runs after the first paint and a remount animated the collapse; steps 1 and 9 drop `accountsArchivedCardA11y`, copy the canvas does not carry.
- The jest `heroui-native` mock (`jest.setup.js:52-230`) has no `Accordion`; nothing in this plan imports `archived_card.tsx` from a test, and it must stay that way.
- `Accordion.Trigger` and `Accordion.Content` carry HeroUI's own padding classes; the `style` zeroing is what `category_budget_row.tsx:40,117` relies on. If the header does not measure 52 on the emulator, that is where to look.
- The `[id]` route on an archived account is MA-049's; before it lands the tap opens a detail that finds no active account. The walk shoots the list, not that screen.
- `unarchiveAccount` reloads the store inside its own `try`, so a restore that wrote but failed to reload reports as a failure here while the row has already moved; the store's `archiveAccount` swallows that reload for the same reason (`account.store.ts:119-122`), `unarchiveAccount` does not, and changing it is MA-047's file.
