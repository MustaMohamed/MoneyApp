## Self-assessment
Steps 3, 4 and 5 are where I am least sure, on one choice: the pay sheet amount, the set-budget limit and the manual rate leave the `Controller`'s `onChange` for a hook action that validates through `setValue` with `shouldValidate: isSubmitted`, the shape the income sheet already ships. I chose it because it puts the field name, the mask and the zero flag where a hook test reaches them, and the pay sheet and set-budget `.tsx` suites then exercise the binding. The cost is a rewritten path for complete values on those fields and a render-time `isSubmitted` read that is easy to get wrong. The account fields, the commitment amount and the plan total keep the inline form because their components are shared or their hook suite stubs the form, so their tests call the helper with the flags by hand and the emulator states carry the binding. A reviewer who wants one pattern can take the inline form everywhere, about 40 lines less, at the price of weaker tests on four fields. The second soft spot is step 2. Its rate effect has to leave a held text alone when a run the clear queued flushes late, Jest cannot stage that order, and only step 7's `fill` walk on the device shows it. The split into two effects also validates a seed twice, which costs a second resolver run and changes no result.
