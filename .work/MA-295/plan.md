## Self-assessment
Step 2 is the one I am least sure of. The open flag now needs both `sheetVisible` and a matching `openOwner`, so every opener has to stamp the owner before it opens; an opener added later that calls `useBudgetState.openAdd` or `openEdit` alone opens nothing; the month-actions and rollover suites catch a dropped stamp only at today's openers. The rule that only `openFor` and `reset` write `openOwner` carries the whole fix: if `release` or `init*` cleared it, a closing mount would close the sheet the next opener just opened.
