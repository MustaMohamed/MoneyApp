### 1. `src/utils/money.ts` exports the whole-percent and exact-comparison helpers
- File: `src/utils/money.ts` (beside `toCents` `:38`, `exceedsToCent` `:48`, `ratioHeldAtTie` `:53`)
- Change: five exported functions. Every argument is a required `number`, none takes `null`, each returns a `number`, and each gets a one-line comment.
  - `wholePercentOf(numerator, denominator)`: both exact integers (cents, counts, days). The size is `Math.floor((2 * |numerator| * 100 + |denominator|) / (2 * |denominator|))` and the sign is `sign(numerator) * sign(denominator)`. It returns positive `0` for a denominator of `0`, as `ratioHeldAtTie` does (`:54`), and for a size of `0`; never `-0`, `NaN` or `Infinity`. No float quotient is rounded.
  - `wholePercent(part, whole)`: `wholePercentOf(toCents(part), toCents(whole))`.
  - `wholePercentGap(part, whole, elapsed, span)`: whole points between `part / whole`, in money, and `elapsed / span`, in whole counts. With `p = toCents(part)` and `w = toCents(whole)` it is `wholePercentOf(p * span - elapsed * w, w * span)`, and `wholePercentOf(-elapsed, span)` when `w` is `0`.
  - `compareToPercent(part, whole, percent)`: `percent` is a whole number. `Math.sign(toCents(part) * 100 - toCents(whole) * percent)`, and `-1` when `toCents(whole) <= 0`.
  - `compareGapToPoints(part, whole, elapsed, span, points)`: `Math.sign((p * span - elapsed * w) * 100 - w * span * points)`, and `-1` when `w <= 0` or `span <= 0`.
  - Outside this file the diff adds `toCents(` lines only in `transactions.helpers.ts`, step 2, which holds five today, and one `exceedsToCent(` in `budget_summary.ts`, step 5. Step 8 records both, and the four helpers here that round through `toCents`, in `docs/adr/2026-08-22-money-rounding-layer.md` §6 (check 1 `:259`, `:273-281`, `:353-356`).
