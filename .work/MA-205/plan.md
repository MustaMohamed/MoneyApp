## Self-assessment
Step 4's after-N branch is the least certain. It tells a Stop from a count end by the settled count alone, since no column records why a commitment went inactive. A Stop that lands after the Nth settlement but before housekeeping ends the commitment reads as a count end, and undo reactivates it. The Decisions lines settle until-date commitments by date and say nothing of this path; the rule follows R4, "the commitment ends when all N are settled", so a commitment with N settled rows counts as ended by count whoever wrote `is_active = 0`.
