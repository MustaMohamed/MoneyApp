## Risks
- Uniwind may emit a `line-height` from HeroUI's `chip__label--size-sm` or `Tabs.Label` class that the inline `style` does not override on Android; MA-086 proved the override on `Chip.Label` (`SuccessChip`) and `Typography` but not on `Tabs.Label`. The compact-tab read (15, centred in 28) is the check; if it fails, the label needs `style` last in the array, not a class change.
- The allocation chip grows from its 32 floor to 33 at 411 dp; if the reviewer reads that as a regression, the alternative is `Type.detail` (16) for the amount, which changes the font and is out of Rules.
- The `Paid` and `Skipped` statuses on the commitments list may be hidden by the default status filter; the recipe's force names the filter, and the implementer confirms it on the emulator rather than skipping the two.
- A plan seed that produces all four spending-plan statuses needs dates on both sides of today; time is not an input on the emulator, so the seed is built against the device date.
- Amended after review: Step 6 now drops `variant="caption"` instead of keeping it, because the Verification census regex matches `variant="caption"` and the gate would have printed `hero_card.tsx:170` at head, failing Acceptance's last line; the 12-file-cap bullet was removed on the reviewer's ruling that recipe markdown does not count.
