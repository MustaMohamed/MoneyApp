## Risks
- With a `type`, `accountIds` or `categoryIds` filter SQLite plans the equality index (`idx_transactions_type (type=?)`; `MULTI-INDEX OR` over `idx_transactions_account_id`/`idx_transactions_to_account_id`; `idx_transactions_category_id`) instead of the date index, in the rows query and in the `JOIN accounts … GROUP BY` aggregate alike (`better-sqlite3` 12.11.1 against the `004_create_transactions.ts:21-34` indexes); the plan tests pin `idx_transactions_date (transaction_date>?` on the month-only and `+ search` shapes, L34's bare date-range chain, and assert `SEARCH … USING INDEX` with no `SCAN transaction_row` on every other shape.
- `matchNetEgp` as a TS fold over day sums can differ from a single SQL SUM in the last ulp on non-integer piastres; the worked numbers are whole EGP, and the agreement case uses `toBe` like its siblings.
- The hook test's `keeps same-month totals visible while revalidating after remount` (`:325`) relies on `preserveData` from `totalsYearMonth`; if the key stamp is used for preservation instead, that case fails and the strip would blank on every keystroke.
- A search keystroke now runs two aggregate statements per debounce (300 ms, `useDebouncedValue`); the previous-month call carries no search joins, so the cost is one indexed GROUP BY plus one indexed SUM.
- Amended after review round 1: the plan assertions in steps 1 and 2 and the gate-proof line now pin `^SEARCH (t|transaction_row) USING INDEX idx_transactions_date (transaction_date>?` on the transactions row, since the base alias `t` and the scan row's index name made the alias-bound form pass at base; step 2's previous-month case seeds a second August expense on the other bank so scoped and unscoped differ; step 5's ADR records that `totalsQueryKey` stamps the request in flight and `days` are current only at `totalsStatus === 'ready'`, for MA-092.
