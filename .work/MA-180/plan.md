## Self-assessment
Step 5 is the least sure. Skeleton and loaded heights agree by construction only if each text's box on Android equals `lineHeightFor` of its app-scaled size. Two places can break that: the StatusBadge label inside HeroUI `Typography`, and the fitted amount line, which RN ceils to a whole pixel. The geometry carries one pixel of slack for the second and none for the first. The `hero, large font` and `skeleton, large font` reads are where a 1 dp gap shows.
