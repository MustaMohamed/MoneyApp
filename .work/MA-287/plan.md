## Risks
- Critical trigger 7: step 1 changes the save path from overwriting a stored limit to refusing; the run stops for the user before that change is pushed.
- `setLimit` writes the name `Budget` through `setBudget`, so a second call for one category and month now throws `BudgetNameTakenError`; its only caller is the store action `budget.store.ts:267`, which no screen calls.
- The sheet's `sessionKey` guard (`set_budget_sheet.hook.ts:104, 121`) fixes the add-mode group at open; a sheet opened before the month's snapshot matches starts on the category default, which is what the tab shows at that moment.
- `budget.md · summary over income`: `text-negative` resolves through `--danger`; if a theme redefines `--danger` apart from `Colors.dark.negative` the two over colours drift.
- `budget.md · summary income figure, large font`: the stacked metric value is capped at `STACKED_METRIC_VALUE_MAX_WIDTH` (50%); a long over amount at 2.0 may wrap.
- `not probed: budget.md · summary income figure, large font`
- `REBUILD:` no files; the probe found the app not installed on emulator slot 2 and ran prebuild, `mqa build` and `mqa install`, so the implementer's first emulator pass on a fresh slot pays the same build.
