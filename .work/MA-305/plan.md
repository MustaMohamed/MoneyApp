## Risks
- `spending_plan_detail.md · Spending card at rest, D2`: D2 draws expense amounts unsigned (`1,500` in `c-exp`) with See all over three rows (MA-336, out of scope); the shipped row signs them `−1,500` and this ticket lists all five, so the shot differs from the frame in both places.
- `spending_plan_detail.md · Spending card, large font`: a wrapped title with `minHeight` lets the row grow past `resolveTransactionRowHeight`; the glyph and tile alignment against a two-line title is the implementer's call and is checked only on the device.
- A bank income in a plan category counts 0 in Spent and so is not listed; a user who calls that a refund will not find it on the card. The ADR states it.
- `TransactionRowBody` is a pressable with a press-scale animation inside `ScreenScroll`; a long plan renders every row unvirtualised.
- `RowCaption` gives the trail (here the category name) the whole-text slot and the lead (the date) the ellipsis; at 2.0 the caption stacks to two lines, which the large-font state checks.
- The hook test mocks expo-router and stores by hand; adding the account store to the hook breaks every existing case until its mock lands in the same commit.
- not probed: spending_plan_detail.md · skeleton, large font
