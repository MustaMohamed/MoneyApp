## Risks
- `spending_plan_detail.md · readable text, large font`: step 5 wraps the hero title and badge above 1.0, but a title that is one word wider than its line still ends in `…` through `resolveLoneWordLines`, since Android breaks such a word at a glyph; `October essentials` is two words.
- Above 1.0 a wrapped title or badge stands the loaded hero taller than its skeleton, which keeps `geometry.height`, so the content below moves down when the load lands; if a hero body reads taller than the geometry at 1.3 or 2.0 with nothing wrapped, its content outgrows the geometry and the `hero, large font` heights (212.19, 266.29) move on transaction and commitment detail.
- `spending_plan_detail.md · active plan, D2`: D2 draws `On track` beside a Watch limit row; the ruling makes the tag Watch, so the shot differs from the frame there by design.
- `spending_plan_detail.md · open across midnight`: the hero rebuilds only through `useLocalDayChange` → `retry`; a reload that fails at midnight leaves yesterday's hero under the floating alert.
- Strong `On pace`: D2 draws only the ahead case; the strong span on `On pace` follows the frame's emphasis on the pace measure and is a field-level choice.
- `Strings` functions returning run arrays are new in `strings.ts`; a lint or structural guard on its value shapes would refuse them.
