### 1. Three shared money helpers exist: zero under half a cent, over to the cent, a ratio held at 1 on a tie to the cent
- File: `src/utils/money.ts` (after `toCents`, `:38-40`)
- Change: three exports. Every argument is a required `number`, none takes `null`.
  - `snapToZero(n: number): number` returns positive `0` when `Math.abs(n) < 0.005`, and `n` unchanged otherwise, so `0.005` and `-0.005` are kept. Never `roundMoney(n) === 0`: half-even zeroes `0.005` (`__tests__/utils/money.test.ts:24-26`), which prints `0.01`.
  - `exceedsToCent(amount: number, limit: number): boolean` is `toCents(amount) > toCents(limit)`, the comparison `sumAllocations` makes at `:57`.
  - `ratioHeldAtTie(part: number, whole: number): number` returns `0` when `whole` is `0`. Otherwise it returns the raw quotient `part / whole`, except that a quotient above `1` comes back as exactly `1` when `exceedsToCent(part, whole)` is false. A quotient at or below `1` is never rounded to cents, so `budgetBandColor` (`src/modules/budget/utils/budget_summary.ts:16-19`) reads `> 1` only for a part over its whole to the cent and reads every lower band on the quotient it reads today.
