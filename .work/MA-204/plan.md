## Self-assessment
Step 2 is the least sure. It carries the carried read, the renamed skip write, the store's `today` and a dashboard repository line in one step because the signature `getPaymentsByMonth(db, yearMonth, today)` has three call sites across two modules and splitting them leaves a step that does not compile; the step is about a third of the diff. Its open detail is the shape of the two-range query: an `OR` of two `due_date` ranges is the shorter SQL and SQLite's OR optimisation should plan it as two index searches, but a four-row test table can make the planner pick a scan and fail the plan assertion for a reason that is not the query's; the `UNION ALL` form avoids that and costs two more lines, and I have named both rather than choosing.
