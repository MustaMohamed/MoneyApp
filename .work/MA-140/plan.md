# MA-140 — Words and amounts readable at a 2.0 font scale; drag cancels the press on the swipe lists' rows; alerts stack their button
base: 66c789d542bac925bd43a9803365920c74f684df · verify: emulator · flags: none · expected diff: ~340 lines

Facts the steps lean on, cited once:

- Android breaks a word wider than its line at a glyph, and `TruncateAt.END` cuts at a glyph; the parent's floor (#602 Goal: whole, or a tail ellipsis inside its own box) and Decision 3 (a lone word: one line, tail ellipsis) are what the code can deliver. A multi-word text is made to fit by giving each of its words a line wide enough, never by a word-aware cut.
- An OS-scaled single line draws its `…` past its box above scale 1 (ADR `docs/adr/2026-09-27-transactions-search-tally.md` §7), so every line this plan caps to one takes `allowFontScaling={false}` and `scaledTextStyle` (`src/components/ui/text_scale.geometry.ts:24-31`); that moves its size from a class to a `Type` token (Rules, bullet 1). On the 411 dp Pixel 2 (`SCALE` 1.055, `src/utils/responsive.ts:10`): `Type.caption` 12.57 over the class's 12 (+4.8%), `Type.title` 18.86 over 18 (+4.8%), `Type.subhead` 16.76 over 16 (+4.8%), `Type.body` 14.77 over 14 (+5.5%), `Type.pillLabel` 10.67 over 10 (+6.7%), all under the 7% bound.
- The threshold for every layout that changes above 1.0 is `fontScale > 1`, the repo's line (`scaledTextStyleAboveOne`, `text_scale.geometry.ts:33-40`; `badgeShrinks`, `transaction_row.tsx:143`; `resolveStateScreenBottomReserve`, `state_screen.geometry.ts:127-129`).
- Jest's window reads a font scale above 1 (`__tests__/screens/commitments/summary_header.test.tsx:109-110` asserts it), so a render case exercises the stacked branch and the geometry tests cover 1.0.
- A row's press: RN `Pressable` fires `onPress` on release inside its retention rect (`Pressability.js:831-870`), and reports movement through `onPressMove` (`Pressable.js:112`, `Pressability.js:483-487`); RNGH's pan activates at `DEFAULT_DRAG_OFFSET` 10 (`react-native-gesture-handler/src/components/ReanimatedSwipeable/ReanimatedSwipeable.tsx:42,506`). HeroUI `PressableFeedback` spreads unknown props onto its `AnimatedPressable` (`heroui-native/src/components/pressable-feedback/pressable-feedback.tsx:51,124-133`), so `onPressMove` reaches RN.

Step 4 is the step I am least sure of. The stat-card header fix rests on a layout claim I cannot measure from the code: that moving the month label onto its own line leaves `MONTH` a line wide enough at 2.0 on a 411 dp phone.
