## Self-assessment
Step 4 is the least sure. `DetailFactRow` puts a fixed `height` on HeroUI `ListGroup.Item` and JS-scaled one-line props on `ListGroup.ItemDescription` and `ItemTitle`, and today's `DetailRow` shows that pattern holds for two stacked lines, but not with a `Chip` beside the value. Whether `Chip size="sm"` keeps its box inside a 44 row at 1.0 and inside the scaled line box at 2.0, and whether the label keeps its width while the value takes the ellipsis, is visible only on the emulator. If the chip's own padding outgrows the line box, the row's height function must take the chip's box as a third term, a change inside `resolveDetailFactRowHeight` that the geometry test absorbs.
