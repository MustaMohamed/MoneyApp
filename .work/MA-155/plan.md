## Self-assessment
Step 8 is the least certain. Its row bans three names, the forms the info rows file held at `origin/main`, while the Acceptance line says "a conversion, a rounding or a `Math` call". A conversion or a rounding written as bare arithmetic passes the row, and no regex row bans that without also failing the file's own comparisons. The Decisions line asks for one row for one file, so the plan stops at the three names and puts the hole under Risks; a reviewer who reads the Acceptance line as wider is asking for a lint rule, which is a different mechanism and its own ticket. Step 7's code is four call swaps with two suites pinning the values. Its open point is the wording of ADR §3, which has to say that the two breakdown helpers convert at an ungated rate: true today, and a money-rules reader may take it as a defect to fix here.
