## Self-assessment
Step 6 is the one I am least sure about. The corner opt-in has two possible mechanisms and I picked the class swap over a bare `style` override because `SegmentFilter` hardcodes `rounded-full` on the track and I did not want the result to depend on RN's style-beats-className precedence for a value the design pins at 12. What I could not check without running it is whether HeroUI's own `.tabs__list` CSS reasserts a radius once `rounded-full` is gone — the non-compact branch's comment (`tabs.tsx:163`) says the scroll view repeats the list's radius in its own CSS, which is why step 5 sets both, but that path never had a competing class to remove. If the track comes back pill-shaped on the emulator, the change is confined to those two literal class strings and step 5's resolver stays as written.
